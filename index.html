Finished Goods QC

Only finished-goods batches marked “Send this batch for QC” appear here. Classify every size separately as Accepted, Rework or Defective. Accepted pieces enter finished stock by size; rework returns to the vendor as a pieces-only job card; defective pieces enter defective inventory. A vendor challan is created for accepted pieces only.

Enter Accepted, Rework and Defective quantities for each size. Rework will create a new vendor job card with a pieces-only quantity.
